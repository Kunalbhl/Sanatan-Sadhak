## Overview

At **Sanatan Sadhak**, we respect your right to privacy and give you complete control over your personal data. This page outlines how you can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.

---

## 1. How to Delete Your Account (In-App)

The fastest and most direct method to delete your account is directly within the Android application:

1. Open **Sanatan Sadhak**.
2. Tap the **Profile** tab on the bottom navigation bar.
3. Open **Account Settings** (⚙️).
4. Tap **Delete Account** (खाता हटाएं).
5. Read the confirmation prompt and tap **Confirm Delete**.

Your account is immediately deleted, your local storage is wiped, and you will be returned to the clean initial setup screen.

---

## 2. What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

When you execute an account deletion:

- **Authentication Records:** Your Firebase Authentication user account and Google Sign-In associations are immediately and permanently erased.
- **Profile Information:** Your display name, email address, profile avatar preferences, and user document in Cloud Firestore are permanently deleted.
- **Personal Sadhana Data:** All Japa mala count histories, meditation timers, daily streaks, karma deeds, and gratitude logs linked to your user ID are purged.
- **Community Posts & Comments:** Your authored community posts and comments will be detached or permanently removed from public view.
- **Bookmarks & Favorites:** Saved scripture verses, stotras, and temple bookmarks are deleted.
- **Local On-Device Cache:** The local Room SQLite database on your device is wiped clean, removing all on-device histories and cached session tokens.

## 4. Data Retention Exceptions

- We **do not retain** personal data after deletion.
- Standard transient server access logs (such as IP addresses in network firewall logs) are automatically purged within 30 days in accordance with standard cloud infrastructure security policies.
