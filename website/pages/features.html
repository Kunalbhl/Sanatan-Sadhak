Every day

🏠 Home & daily sadhana

Your day starts with a greeting, today's date, the Panchang at a glance, quick actions and the shloka of the day.

  1. Open the app. The banner greets you with the deity of the day.
  2. Read today's tithi, nakshatra, sunrise, sunset and Rahu Kaal in the Panchang card.
  3. Tap a Quick Action: Japa Mala, Dhyan, My Mantra, Aarti or Chants.
  4. Scroll to Daily Shloka & Wisdom to read the meaning or tap to listen.
{{motif:mala right}}
Core tool

📿 Japa & mantra counter

A big, calm tap target for counting chants, with mala tracking, daily targets and streaks.

  1. Open Sadhana → Bhakti Tools → Mantra Counter.
  2. Choose a mantra from Select Mantra. Its Sanskrit text appears above the counter.
  3. Tap the circle for each chant. There's a soft vibration at every 108.
  4. Switch to Japa Timer to chant for a set time instead.
  5. Your mala count and day streak (🔥) update automatically.
{{motif:lotus left}}
Listen & chant

🎶 Mantra library, My Mantras & chant player

Classical chants with lyrics, meaning and audio. Add your own mantras and keep a background Om chant playing.

  1. Open Sadhana → Prayers and search for any chalisa, aarti or stotram.
  2. Open a chant to read its lyrics and meaning and press play.
  3. Use the audio queue to line up several tracks.
  4. Tap Add Personal Prayer to save your own mantra in My Mantras.
  5. Turn on Global Background Chant in Profile → App Preferences for ambient Om.

Ritual

🔔 Aarti bell

Ring a sacred temple bell during your aarti. It sounds and vibrates, and every ring is logged.

  1. Open Bhakti Tools → Aarti Bell.
  2. Tap the bell to ring it during your aarti.
  3. See your ring history in Bell Ring Logs, and clear it any time.

Peace

🧘 Dhyana meditation

A breath and meditation timer with ambient Om, flute or temple bells, and goals to keep you consistent.

  1. Open Sadhana → Dhyana Sadhana.
  2. Pick a duration and an ambient sound, and set the volume.
  3. Press Start. The screen goes calm and focused.
  4. Finish to log the session towards your meditation goal.

Commitment

🙏 Sankalpa & Gayatri Anushthan

Take a vow (sankalpa) with a target and duration, follow guided anushthan, and track each day.

  1. Open Sadhana → Dashboard → Active Sankalpa.
  2. Choose a sankalpa, for example 11 malas a day for 40 days, or Gayatri Anushthan.
  3. Log each day's practice. Progress and streak show on the dashboard.
  4. Complete it, or start a new one when you're ready.

Reflect

🌱 Karma & vow tracker, gratitude, journal

Log good deeds and seva, write daily gratitude, and keep a personal spiritual journal. Karma points reward consistency.

  1. Open Bhakti Tools → Karma Tracker and tap Log Deed.
  2. Open Gratitude and write three things you're thankful for.
  3. Use the Personal Spiritual Journal for longer reflections.
  4. Watch Karma Points and the Karma Streak grow on Home and Profile.

Guidance

🪔 Pooja vidhi & prayers

Step-by-step puja procedures with samagri lists, plus lyrics for aartis, chalisas and stotras.

  1. Open Knowledge → Pooja & Vidhi, or a deity page.
  2. Read the samagri list and gather the items.
  3. Follow each numbered step of the vidhi.
  4. Open Prayer Lyrics to sing along.

Live

🗓️ Panchang & Hindi calendar

Tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana, plus sunrise and sunset, Rahu Kaal, Abhijit, Choghadiya, festivals and Ekadashi for your location. It's calculated in the sidereal zodiac with Lahiri ayanamsa.

  1. Tap the calendar icon at the top, or View Full Panchang on Home.
  2. Set your city or use GPS (Current GPS / Custom Location).
  3. Read the Detailed Panchang, Auspicious & Inauspicious Muhurtas and the Day Choghadiya.
  4. Open the Monthly Calendar for festivals and vrats such as Ekadashi.
  5. You can also try the live Panchang on this website.

Learn

📜 Knowledge hub / Vedic library

A library of Sanatan Dharma: Vedas, Upanishads, Itihasa, deities, temples and puja, with verified sources and reading time.

  1. Open Knowledge from the bottom bar.
  2. Filter by category or sacred deity (Shiva, Vishnu, Krishna…).
  3. Sort by difficulty, format or newest.
  4. Read an article and bookmark it. Your reading progress is saved.

Darshan

🛕 Temple finder

A sacred mandir directory: the 12 Jyotirlingas, Char Dham and city temples, with distance, story, directions and sharing.

  1. Open Bhakti Tools → Temple Finder.
  2. Pick a reference city (Varanasi, Vrindavan, Ayodhya, Haridwar) or use your location.
  3. Filter by deity or search by name.
  4. Open a temple for its story, then tap Directions or Share.

Ask

✨ AI Mitra

A gentle AI companion for questions on granths, rituals, deities and dharma. Answers are short, with something to try today and links into the app.

  1. Tap Ask AI Mitra on Home or open Chat.
  2. Pick a suggested prompt or type your question in English or Hindi.
  3. Tap the chips under an answer to jump to Panchang, Knowledge or Mantras.
  4. Mitra is an AI: for rituals and important decisions, consult a qualified acharya.

Personal

🌗 Profile, language & themes

Your devotee profile, stats and settings: English or Hindi, light or dark, notifications and account controls.

  1. Open Profile from the bottom bar and sign in.
  2. Tap EN | हि at the top to switch language at any time.
  3. Open App Preferences & Theme for light or dark mode, sounds and the background chant.
  4. Edit your name and photo, or delete your account.

No account needed

🕊️ Guest mode

Use Japa, Panchang, meditation, the library, temples and audio without signing in. Sign in later to sync.

  1. Open the app and tap Not Now on the sign-in prompt.
  2. Use any tool. Your progress stays on this phone.
  3. Community and Mitra will ask you to sign in.
  4. Sign in later to sync your progress.
