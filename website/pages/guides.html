🚀 Getting started

Install, choose your language, and decide whether to sign in.

Open the app. Sign in with Google or email to sync, or tap Not Now to continue as a guest.

Pick your language. Tap EN | हि at the top right to switch between English and Hindi at any time.

Check today's Panchang on Home. Tap View Full Panchang to set your city.

Explore the bottom bar: Knowledge, Sadhana, Home, Videos and Profile.

Commitment

🙏 Set your sankalpa

A sankalpa is a vow of practice. The app keeps count so you can focus on the practice itself.

Open Sadhana and tap the Dashboard chip.

Chant each day with the Mantra Counter. It counts towards your sankalpa automatically.

Core tool

📿 Use the japa counter

On Home, tap Japa Mala in Quick Actions, or go to Sadhana → Bhakti Tools.

Under Select Mantra, choose a mantra such as the Gayatri Mantra. Its text appears above the counter.

Tap the circle for every chant. You'll feel a gentle vibration at each completed mala of 108.

Your Daily Japa Sadhana target and 🔥 day streak update by themselves.

🗓️ Read the Panchang

The five limbs (panch-ang) of the day: tithi, vara (weekday), nakshatra, yoga and karana. Try them in the live Panchang.

Tithi is the lunar day: 30 in a month, Shukla (waxing) then Krishna (waning). Vrats like Ekadashi follow the tithi at sunrise.

✨ Nakshatra
Shravana · Pada 2Moon at 283.5° sidereal
27 nakshatras × 13°20′

Nakshatra is the Moon's lunar mansion, measured in the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri ayanamsa).

⏱️ Muhurat
Rahu Kaalavoid new work
Abhijitmidday, auspicious

Rahu Kaal is avoided for new beginnings. Abhijit and the good Choghadiya slots are favoured.

📍 Location

Timings depend on where you are. Set your city or GPS for accurate sunrise and muhurat.
