Everything your practice needs

Built for devotees: works in English and Hindi, much of it works offline, and there are no ads.

📿

Japa & mala counter

Tap or use the big counter to chant. It tracks malas, daily targets and streaks, with gentle sound and vibration feedback.

🎶

Mantras & stotras

Gayatri, Mahamrityunjaya, Hanuman Chalisa, Vishnu Sahasranamam and more, with meaning, lyrics, audio and your own custom mantras.

🗓️

Panchang & festivals

Daily tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, Choghadiya, Rahu Kaal and muhurat for your location, plus a monthly Hindu calendar of vrats and festivals.

🛕

Temple finder & darshan

Sacred temples like Kashi Vishwanath, Kedarnath, Jagannath and Tirupati, with directions and darshan videos.

📜

Vedic library

Articles on dharma, deities, puja vidhi and scriptures. Filter by devta, bookmark what you love and pick up where you left off.

🪔

Mitra guide

Ask Sadhak Mitra about granths, rituals or karma and get short, kind answers with something to try today. It's an AI companion, not a guru.

🧘

Dhyana & breath

A meditation timer with ambient Om, flute or temple bells, and goals to keep you consistent.

🙏

Sankalpa & karma journal

Set a vow or anushthan, log good deeds and gratitude, and watch your karma streak grow.

👥

Satsang community

Share reflections, like and discuss with fellow sadhaks in a respectful, moderated space.

How it works

Two minutes to set up, and then a few quiet minutes each day.

Install & sign in

Use it as a guest, or sign in with Google to sync your progress.

Choose your path

Pick your mantras, ishta devta and a sankalpa to begin.

Practise daily

Chant, meditate and check today's Panchang and muhurat.

Learn & share

Read, ask Mitra and join satsang with other sadhaks.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Sanatan Sadhak is free and has no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can use japa, Panchang, meditation, the library and audio. Signing in with Google adds sync, the community and Mitra.

Is iOS coming?

We're starting with Android. Join early access and we'll tell you about other platforms.

How is my data handled?

We collect only what the app needs, never sell your data and let you delete everything. Read the privacy policy or delete your account.
