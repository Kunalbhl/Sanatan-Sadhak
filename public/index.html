Every day

Home & daily sadhana

  1. Open the app to a greeting, today's date and the deity of the day.
  2. See today's Panchang: tithi, nakshatra, sunrise, sunset and Rahu Kaal.
  3. Use Quick Actions to jump into Japa Mala, Dhyan, My Mantras, Aarti or Chants.
  4. Scroll down for the daily shloka with meaning and listen-aloud.
Core tool

Japa counter

  1. Go to Bhakti Tools, then Mantra Counter.
  2. Choose your mantra. Its Sanskrit text is shown while you chant.
  3. Tap the big circle for each chant. You'll feel a vibration at every 108.
  4. Your mala count and day streak are saved automatically.
Listen & chant

Mantras & prayers

  1. Open Sadhana and pick Prayers, My Mantras or Dhyana Sadhana.
  2. Search for any aarti, chalisa or stotram.
  3. Open a chant to read lyrics and meaning and play the audio.
  4. Add your own prayer with "Add Personal Prayer".
Darshan

Temple finder

  1. Open Bhakti Tools, then Temple Finder.
  2. Pick a holy city (Varanasi, Vrindavan, Ayodhya, Haridwar) or use your location.
  3. Filter by deity and read each temple's story and distance.
  4. Tap Directions to open maps, or Share to send it to family.
Learn

Knowledge hub

  1. Open Knowledge from the bottom bar.
  2. Filter by category (Vedas, Upanishads, Itihasa) or by deity.
  3. Sort by difficulty, format or newest. Every article shows its source.
  4. Bookmark articles and pick up where you left off.
You

Profile & sign-in

  1. Use the app as a guest, or sign in with Google or email.
  2. Signing in syncs your streaks and unlocks Community and Mitra.
  3. Change language, theme and notifications in Profile.
  4. Delete your account any time. It's in Profile, or at /delete-account.

Questions

Is Sanatan Sadhak free?

Yes. It's free and has no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. As a guest you can use japa, Panchang, meditation, the library and audio. Sign in to sync and to use Community and Mitra.

How accurate is the Panchang?

It calculates real Sun and Moon positions (to within about 2 arcminutes) in the sidereal zodiac with Lahiri ayanamsa. Tithi is taken at local sunrise. For major ceremonies, confirm with your purohit.

Which languages are supported?

English and Hindi, with Sanskrit text for mantras and shlokas.

Is iOS coming?

We're starting with Android. Join early access and we'll tell you about other platforms.

How is my data handled?

We collect only what the app needs and never sell it. See the privacy policy, or delete your account at any time.
