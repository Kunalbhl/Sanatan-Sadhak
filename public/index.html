Effective Date: August 12, 2026

Sanatan Sadhak - Daily Panchang is a private, offline-first spiritual companion application designed for Sanatan Dharma practitioners. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent data practices. This policy details how we handle user information.

Core Philosophy: We operate primarily on an offline-first model. Your core spiritual habits, journals, and counts are your private sacred domain.

1. Location Data & Offline Calculations

To deliver high-precision daily Vedic Panchang coordinates (including local Tithi, Nakshatra, Sunrise, Sunset, and Rahu Kalam timings), the app may request permission to access your device's fine or coarse location coordinates:

  • Local Calculations: All location data is processed strictly locally on-device. No location coordinate payloads are ever transmitted to our cloud endpoints, stored on servers, or tracked over time.
  • Privacy Safeguard: Location permissions are completely optional. If denied, the app gracefully falls back to calculated or preset parameters without interrupting overall system functionality.

2. User Accounts & Identity Sync

When you create an authentication profile or log in to the "Sadhak Mandir":

  • Data Collected: We process your Name and Email address via Firebase Authentication and store profile preferences securely in Cloud Firestore.
  • Purpose: This profile allows you to sync your community posts, comments, moderation requests, and administrative access safely across devices.
  • Data Isolation: User profiles are strictly isolated. No database records are ever rented, sold, or shared with third-party marketing companies.

3. Local Storage & Offline Room Database

All core sadhana, progress logs, and personal habits remain securely on your device:

  • Offline Records: Daily Japa counts, Japa Mala history, spiritual journaling logs, custom target milestones, and favorite mantras are stored strictly in the local SQLite Room Database.
  • Zero Cloud Tracking: These items are never sent to external servers unless you specifically opt to post in the public community forum.

4. Sadhak AI Mitra & Gemini Processing

The "Sadhak AI Mitra" assistant provides secure, context-aware interactive spiritual guidance:

  • Secure REST Delivery: Prompt questions are routed safely through secure, encrypted endpoints using official Google Gemini APIs.
  • Privacy Isolation: No personal details, names, or email accounts are attached to the API prompts.
  • Scriptural Safeguards: AI Mitra acts under strict boundaries that separate authentic scriptural source text from AI-generated traditional interpretation, avoiding the fabrication of Sanskrit verses.

5. Devotional Videos & YouTube API

The "Bhakti Video Hub" retrieves curated classical devotional music, audio, and high-quality chanting videos:

  • Retrieved Data: Content is loaded via the official YouTube Data API and RSS feeds of designated, authentic channels.
  • Zero tracking: Our integration does not track your private viewing history or build behavioral advertisement profiles. All cache data resides inside the local database.

6. User-Generated Content (UGC) & Moderation

The community feed allows devotees to connect, share wisdom, and view spiritual announcements:

  • Posting Guidelines: Users can create custom posts and submit supportive comments. Community guidelines are displayed and easily accessible within the post creation screen.
  • Reporting & Safety: Any user can immediately flag or report inappropriate posts. Reported content reaches the moderated admin workspace immediately for swift review.
  • Admin Enforcement: Administrators hold authoritative server-side rights to instantly review, block, or delete offending UGC posts. Non-admin users are strictly prevented from executing moderation writes.

7. Security, Encryption & Network Protocol

To keep your authentication and community communications protected:

  • All network communications are strictly enforced over encrypted HTTPS / TLS 1.3 channels.
  • The application disables cleartext HTTP traffic to prevent any potential interception or tampering on public Wi-Fi networks.

8. Complete Account Deletion Right

In accordance with Google Play Store policies, we support full self-service account deletion:

  • How to Delete: Navigate to your Profile -> Settings screen and click the Delete Devotee Account button.
  • Deletion Scope: Upon confirmation, our system instantly triggers:
    • Deletion of your Firebase Authentication record.
    • Deletion of your public/private Cloud Firestore profile document and metadata.
    • Complete local purge of offline databases, preferences, and session caches.
  • Content Preservation: For community continuity, public spiritual commentary or shared community posts are anonymous-disassociated rather than leaving empty gaps, unless explicitly removed.
