Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Applies to the Sanatan Sadhak Android app and sanatan-sadhak.chaloone.com

Sanatan Sadhak ("the app", "we", "us") is built and run by K. Tech Services, a Udyam-registered MSME owned by Kunal Pareek in Rajasthan, India. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is stored and how you can delete it. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and Google Play's User Data policy.

In short: you can use most of the app without an account. If you sign in, we store your name, email and the content you create so it can sync. We don't sell your data or show ads, and you can delete everything at any time.

2. How we use it

  • To run the features you use: sign-in, sync, community, Panchang, notifications and Mitra.
  • To keep the community safe: moderation, abuse reports and blocking spam.
  • To send the daily reminders you turned on (prayer, Panchang, shloka). You can switch them off in the app or in Android settings.
  • To fix bugs, prevent fraud and improve the app, using aggregated or technical data.
  • To reply when you contact support.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time, together with the legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act.

3. Location

Sunrise, tithi times, Rahu Kaal and muhurat depend on where you are. If you grant location permission, coordinates are used on your device to calculate the Panchang and temple distances. If the optional online Panchang service is enabled, only the coordinates and the date are sent to it, without any account identifier. You can type a city instead, or refuse permission and use a default location.

4. Mitra (AI guide)

Messages you send to Mitra are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. Please don't share sensitive personal details in chat. Mitra is an AI companion: its answers can be wrong and are not scripture, religious authority, or medical, legal or financial advice. For rituals and important decisions, consult a qualified acharya or professional.

5. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Hosting): accounts, sync, community and security. Firebase privacy.
  • Google Gemini API: Mitra replies.
  • YouTube: darshan and bhajan videos are embedded from YouTube, which may set its own cookies when you play them. Google privacy policy.
  • Instagram: the Divine Updates feed embeds public posts from Instagram.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and may store it outside India.

6. Sharing & sale

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and the app contains no advertising SDKs. We disclose data only to the providers above, when the law requires it, or to protect users from harm. Your community posts, display name and photo are visible to other users by design.

7. Storage & security

All connections use HTTPS/TLS. Firestore security rules limit each account to its own private data, and only admins can change roles. Local data stays in the app's private storage on your device. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India if a breach occurs, as the law requires.

8. Retention & deletion

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account, or online at sanatan-sadhak.chaloone.com/delete-account.
  • Deletion removes your login, profile, synced data and your posts and comments within 30 days. Backups and logs expire within a further 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can access a summary of your data, correct it, delete it, withdraw consent, name a nominee, and raise a grievance with us. To use any of these rights, email support@chaloone.com. We reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

The app is meant for a general audience. Users under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian's consent, as the DPDP Act requires. We don't knowingly collect children's data without that consent or track children for advertising. If you believe a child has given us data without consent, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. This website

This site doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. When you submit the early-access, contact or deletion forms, we store what you type (name, email, city, message) in Cloud Firestore, only to reply to you or act on your request. Early-access emails are deleted after launch or on request. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for important changes we'll tell you in the app.
