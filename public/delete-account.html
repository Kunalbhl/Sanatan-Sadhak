Delete your account & data

Sanatan Sadhak by K. Tech Services · Last updated: 16 November 2026

You can delete your Sanatan Sadhak account and its data at any time. Use whichever option suits you.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sanatan Sadhak and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and the data on your phone is cleared.

Option 2: Request it here

If you no longer have the app, send this form. We'll confirm by email from support@chaloone.com, check that the request comes from the account owner, and finish within 30 days.

Or email support@chaloone.com with the subject "Sanatan Sadhak – Delete my account".

What gets deleted

  • Your login (Firebase Authentication) and profile: name, email, photo and role.
  • Synced sadhana data: japa and mala counts, streaks, karma points and favourites.
  • Your community posts, comments and likes.
  • Data on your device: the local database, journals, custom mantras and settings.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Security and server logs, which expire automatically within 30 days.
  • A record that a deletion request was completed (your email and the date), kept for up to 1 year to show we met the request. Nothing else is kept.
  • Anything we must keep by law, for example an active legal complaint, only for as long as the law requires.
