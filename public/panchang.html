Today's Panchang

Live tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, Rahu Kaal and muhurat for your city.

🌙

Sidereal, not tropical

Indian panchangs measure the Moon against the stars. We take the true Sun and Moon positions and subtract the Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa, about 24.2° in 2026.

🌅

Udaya tithi

The tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana that prevail at local sunrise name the day. That's the rule used to set vrats like Ekadashi.

📍

Your location matters

Sunrise, Rahu Kaal and muhurats shift with latitude and longitude. Choose your city or tap "My location".
